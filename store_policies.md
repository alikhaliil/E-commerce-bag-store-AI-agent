# Haqiba Bags — Store Policies & FAQ

Haqiba Bags is an online bag store based in Egypt. This document is the official reference for shipping, returns, warranty, payments, working hours and customer support escalation. All prices are in Egyptian Pounds (EGP).

## Shipping Policy

### Delivery Areas
We deliver to all 27 governorates in Egypt. We do not currently ship outside Egypt. Orders are delivered by our courier partners, and the customer receives an SMS with the tracking number as soon as the order is shipped.

### Delivery Times
- Cairo, Giza and Alexandria: 2–3 business days from order confirmation.
- All other governorates (Delta, Canal cities, Upper Egypt): 4–5 business days from order confirmation.
- Remote areas (North and South Sinai, Red Sea, Matrouh, New Valley): 5–7 business days.

Business days are Saturday to Thursday. Orders confirmed after 4:00 PM, or on Fridays and official public holidays, are processed on the next business day.

### Shipping Fees
- Cairo, Giza and Alexandria: 60 EGP.
- Other governorates: 85 EGP.
- Remote areas: 120 EGP.

### Free Shipping
Shipping is free on all orders with a subtotal of 2,000 EGP or more, after any discounts are applied. Free shipping applies to all governorates, including remote areas.

### Order Tracking
Customers can track an order with the order number (for example ORD-1004) through our support assistant, or with the tracking number on the courier's website. Tracking numbers start with "EGX" and become available once the order status is "shipped".

### Delayed Orders
If an order has not arrived within 2 business days after the estimated delivery window, it is considered delayed. For delayed orders we will:
- Contact the courier and share an updated delivery date with the customer within 24 hours.
- Refund the shipping fee automatically if the delay is caused by us or our courier.
- Allow the customer to cancel a delayed order that has not yet been shipped, with a full refund.

## Order Status Guide

- **Pending**: the order was placed and is waiting for confirmation (for Instapay orders, waiting for the payment to be verified).
- **Processing**: the order is confirmed and is being prepared and packed in our warehouse.
- **Shipped**: the order was handed to the courier and has a tracking number.
- **Delivered**: the courier confirmed delivery to the customer.
- **Delayed**: the order is behind its estimated delivery date. Our team is already following up with the courier or warehouse.
- **Cancelled**: the order was cancelled by the customer or by the store. Any payment is refunded according to the refund timeline.
- **Returned**: the item was returned to our warehouse and the refund or exchange is being processed.

Orders can be cancelled free of charge while their status is "pending" or "processing". Once an order is "shipped", it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery under the return policy.

## Return & Exchange Policy

### Return Window
Customers can return any eligible item within 14 days of the delivery date. To start a return, contact customer support with the order number and the reason for the return.

### Return Conditions
To be eligible for a return, the item must be:
- Unused, unwashed and in the same condition it was received in.
- Returned with all original tags attached, the dust bag, and the original packaging.
- Free of stains, scratches, odours or any signs of wear.

### Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned or exchanged unless they arrive damaged or defective:
- Personalised or monogrammed bags.
- Items bought during clearance sales marked "Final Sale".
- Gift cards.

### Return Shipping Costs
- If the item is defective, damaged on arrival, or we sent the wrong item, return shipping is free and our courier will collect it.
- For all other returns (for example, a change of mind), a 60 EGP pickup fee is deducted from the refund.

### Refund Timeline
Refunds are issued after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, which takes up to 2 business days.
- Credit/debit card payments: refunded to the same card within 7–14 business days, depending on the bank.
- Instapay payments: refunded to the same Instapay account within 3–5 business days.
- Cash on Delivery orders: refunded by Instapay or mobile wallet within 5 business days. The customer must provide the account details to support.

The original shipping fee is not refundable, except when the item was defective, damaged or wrong.

### Exchanges
Items can be exchanged for a different colour or a different model within the same 14-day window, subject to stock availability. If the new item costs more, the customer pays the difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded using the refund timeline above. The first exchange on an order has no shipping fee.

### Damaged or Wrong Items
If an item arrives damaged or is not the item that was ordered, the customer must report it within 48 hours of delivery, with clear photos of the item and the packaging. We will offer a free replacement or a full refund, including the shipping fee.

## Warranty & Care

### Warranty Coverage
All Haqiba Bags products come with a 6-month warranty from the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in:
- Zippers and zipper pulls.
- Stitching and seams.
- Buckles, clasps and metal hardware.

### Warranty Exclusions
The warranty does not cover:
- Normal wear and tear, such as fading, scuffing or softening of materials over time.
- Damage caused by misuse, overloading the bag beyond its intended capacity, or accidents.
- Cuts, burns, stains or water damage to items that are not described as waterproof.
- Bags that were repaired or altered by anyone other than Haqiba Bags.

### How to Make a Warranty Claim
Contact customer support with the order number, a short description of the problem and clear photos of the defect. Our team reviews claims within 3 business days. Approved claims are repaired free of charge or, if a repair is not possible, the item is replaced with the same or an equivalent model. Warranty pickups and deliveries are free.

### Leather Care
- Wipe leather with a soft, dry cloth after use. For dirt, use a slightly damp cloth and let the bag dry naturally, away from direct sunlight and heaters.
- Apply a leather conditioner every 2–3 months to keep the leather soft and prevent cracking.
- Do not use alcohol, bleach, or household cleaners on leather.
- Store leather bags stuffed with paper inside the dust bag to keep their shape. Do not store them in plastic bags, as leather needs to breathe.

### Canvas, Nylon & Waterproof Bags
- Canvas totes can be machine washed on a cold, gentle cycle and air dried. Do not tumble dry.
- Clean nylon and waterproof bags with mild soap and lukewarm water. Do not machine wash bags that have a laptop sleeve or padded panels.
- Empty and air dry waterproof bags fully before storing them to prevent odours.

## Payment Methods

### Cash on Delivery (COD)
Cash on Delivery is available in all governorates for orders up to 10,000 EGP. Customers pay the courier in cash when the order arrives. Please have the exact amount ready, as couriers may not carry change.

### Instapay
Customers can pay by Instapay transfer at checkout. The order stays in "pending" status until the transfer is verified, which takes up to 2 hours during working hours. If the transfer is not received within 24 hours, the order is cancelled automatically.

### Credit & Debit Cards
We accept Visa, Mastercard and Meeza cards through a secure payment gateway. Card details are never stored by Haqiba Bags. Instalment plans through partner banks are available on orders above 3,000 EGP.

### Payment Issues
If a customer was charged but did not receive an order confirmation, they should contact support with the transaction reference. Duplicate or failed charges are usually reversed automatically by the bank within 7 business days. If not, our team escalates the case to the payment provider.

## Store Working Hours & Contact

### Working Hours
- Customer support (human agents): Saturday to Thursday, 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM, and Friday, 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM (Cairo time).
- The AI support assistant is available 24/7 for order tracking, product questions and policy questions.
- Our warehouse dispatches orders Saturday to Thursday. There are no dispatches on Fridays or official public holidays.

### Contact Channels
Customers can reach us through the website chat, WhatsApp, or email. Messages received outside working hours are answered at the start of the next working period.

## Escalation Policy

### When We Escalate to a Human Agent
The AI support assistant handles routine questions. A support ticket is created and the conversation is escalated to a human agent when:
- The customer explicitly asks to speak to a human.
- An item arrived damaged, defective or wrong.
- There is a payment problem, such as a charge without an order or a missing refund.
- An order is delayed beyond the delivery window and the customer wants a cancellation, refund or compensation.
- A refund or return request falls outside the standard policy and needs a manager's approval.
- The customer is very upset, or the issue was not solved after two attempts.

### Ticket Priorities & Response Times
- **Urgent**: payment charged without an order, or a safety issue. First response within 2 working hours.
- **High**: damaged, defective or wrong items, and delayed orders. First response within 4 working hours.
- **Medium**: return, exchange and warranty requests. First response within 24 working hours.
- **Low**: general questions and feedback. First response within 48 working hours.

### What the Customer Receives
When a ticket is created, the customer receives a ticket number. They can use it to follow up on the case through any contact channel. Customers are notified by SMS or WhatsApp whenever the ticket status changes.
